## Verdict

**This is an unusually well-built engine wrapped around a game that has not been finished.** The deterministic layer is real and correct: `src/engine/` is 1,094 lines of pure, tested, seeded rules; the save layer does IndexedDB transactions with checksums and versioned migrations; the content pack holds 35 quests, 30 NPCs, 240 authored dialogue lines, 9 encounters and 12 advanced job branches, all validated at boot. The recently-shipped work is genuinely shipped — I confirmed difficulty flags, boss signature skills, vendor buy/sell, the full stat line, agility-fed evasion, three endings with faction consequences and three recruits with exclusive skills all exist in code and are reachable. Nothing in the "recently completed" list is vapor. `npx vitest run` is green at 141/141 across 23 files, and `npx tsx tools/campaign-simulation.ts` walks all 35 quests and wins all three boss fights on all eight seeds.

**The honest headline gap is that almost none of it reaches the player, and the parts that do are not yet a JRPG.** The pattern repeats at every layer: `getInitiativeOrder` sorts by agility and has exactly one reference in the repo — its own definition, so turn order is array index forever. `failQuest` and `resetFailedQuest` have zero callers. `reserve` is threaded through shared types, engine state and the Zod schema and is never written. The `ice` element exists in two type declarations and nowhere else. `BattleActorView.status` is populated and read by nothing. `levelsGained` is computed and dropped at both call sites. Every enemy in the game is `boss ? { nature: 0.2, fire: -0.2 } : { nature: -0.1 }` — that is the entire elemental identity of a game with eleven elements. All four healing skills are `target: "self"`, so the dedicated healer job can only heal itself. There is no target selection, no MP display in battle, no animation, no damage numbers, no music, no cutscene structure (`DialogueCatalog` is literally `Record<string, readonly string[]>`), no interactable object anywhere in nine locations, no map, no NG+ and no postgame. And the simulation I ran confirms the balance failure precisely: `kiln-heart` and `varn-rootless` both resolve in **2 rounds**, so the authored six-phase boss choreography — the best content in the game — never plays. On top of that sit four defects that destroy player data outright: `quickSave` defaults to `F5` with no `preventDefault`, so the advertised save key reloads the browser mid-IndexedDB-write; `children.removeAll()` in both scenes orphans a canvas and a game-scoped texture per Text per repaint; the defeat path revives the party at 50% HP **and then calls `persist("autosave")`**, destroying the pre-wipe state; and `NEW CHRONICLE` overwrites a completed run with no confirmation. Fix those four and the widened presentation contract, and roughly half of this backlog becomes cheap wiring rather than new construction.

> **Correction, retracted during Wave 1.** This document originally claimed the "hard difficulty flakes CI about half the time" finding did not reproduce. **That retraction was wrong and is itself now retracted.** Measured during Wave 1 implementation: the solo starting party loses `encounter.mossroad-foragers` on hard **52.5% of the time** (63/120 trials), against 0/120 on easy and normal. `tests/integration/EngineGameBridge.test.ts` really was failing about half of all runs — confirmed at 5/8 failures, and reproduced on the pre-Wave-1 tree at 2/8, so it predates any of this work. The original auditor was right; the verifier that overturned it reasoned from a green single run.
>
> The part of the retraction that stands: `tools/campaign-simulation.ts` genuinely has no difficulty axis, so no hard-difficulty case is under simulation. Both halves of the finding are real. Wave 1 made the test deterministic by pinning the chronicle seed (difficulty is now the only variable); the underlying solo-party action-economy asymmetry is untouched and remains **#43**.

### Deliberate omissions

Carried forward verbatim from the merge stage. Not backlogged; I do not override any of them.

- **No combo, chain, tech, or limit-break system that escalates across a fight.** Coherent for this game's shape. `CombatAction` is attack|skill|guard, `CombatState` carries only party/enemies/rng/round/outcome, and `Combatant` has no per-battle resource beyond MP — a deliberate, small, fully deterministic surface, judged intentional at polish severity by both verifiers. A limit gauge exists to give twenty-round fights escalating texture; this campaign has nine encounters and three bosses, and the fights are currently too short rather than too long. Every other item in this list adds decisions to a round; a charge meter adds a decision to a fight, which is the wrong layer to fix first. If boss fights are rebalanced to eight-plus rounds and still feel flat, revisit then — the cheapest form is a per-combatant `focus` counter on `Combatant` incremented in `advanceCombatRound` plus an optional `focusCost` on `CombatSkill`, and it stays available.
- **No formation, row, or party-reordering mechanic.** `GAME_DESIGN.md:33` states the intended combat model is positionless, and a flat four-versus-N encounter is coherent for a small offline browser JRPG with a 9-piece gear catalog and 9 locations — Chrono Trigger ships without rows. Adding `row` to `PlayerCharacter` would also drag in a save migration and a targeting-bias rule for no decision the player currently lacks a vocabulary for. The genuine sting — that party order is completely inert — is fully addressed by wiring `getInitiativeOrder` (#46). Revisit only if front/back-line tactics become a stated design goal.
- **No crafting, upgrading, or enhancement system.** The finding itself concedes FF4 and Chrono Trigger ship without crafting, and a new `src/engine/crafting.ts` plus recipe data plus a Hett Copper UI is a large system whose two stated justifications — key-item clutter and a late-game currency sink — are each answered more cheaply by items already on this list (#92 and #75/#83). `hasItems` in `src/engine/inventory.ts` remains unused, a small tolerable cost. Genuinely optional scope, not an absence a player of this game would feel.
- **The main quest chain auto-starts and cannot be declined.** Auto-advancing the linear 15-quest main chain in `advanceCampaign` is deliberate softlock protection: a player who declines the critical path in a nine-location world with no alternate route has no way to resume it. The half a player genuinely feels — side quests being assigned rather than offered, with no acknowledgement — is covered by #14, which announces acquisition through the already-declared `startedQuestId`. An explicit Accept/Decline prompt on top buys little for a chain the player must follow anyway, and I am consciously not proposing it.
- **No achievement or milestone system.** None of the reference-class JRPGs this project benchmarks against shipped achievements, and building an evaluation pass over world flags would add engine surface for no gameplay return. The one part worth keeping — the persisted `chronicle`, currently reduced to a single hint line — is folded into #31/#102 as a scrollable journal section, so the substrate gets surfaced without an achievement system existing.

## Structural decisions required

Answer these before implementation starts. Recommendations are mine; the reasoning is what matters if you disagree.

### A. Contract and schema shape (Wave 2 — blocks everything)

1. **Does the target cursor reset each turn or remember the last target?** → **Remember, reset on target death.** Genre convention (FF/DQ both do it), and it removes a keypress from every repeat attack in a three-enemy fight.
2. **Is initiative recomputed per round or fixed at battle start?** → **Per round.** Fixed order makes haste/slow (#12) meaningless the moment you ship them, and re-derivation is cheap. Cost: a landed stun changes order mid-fight, which is the point.
3. **Do player characters also get `statusResistance`, or only enemies?** → **Both, but author only enemies now.** Make the field live on `Combatant` (it already is, structurally), populate it from equipment in #60, and leave player values empty in v1. Adding it later is a second migration.
4. **How large should the buff/debuff set be?** → **Exactly four: weaken, fortify, haste, slow.** Symmetric, covers the Trickster's stated identity, and keeps the persisted union small. Resist adding a fifth until a job needs it.
5. **Does enemy AI variation stay deterministic (round-derived) or consume `state.rng`?** → **Round-derived.** Consuming RNG changes every existing seeded test and breaks simulation reproducibility for zero player-visible gain — a player cannot tell a round-derived pick from a random one.
6. **Ice: retheme or remove?** → **Retheme.** Removal touches `src/shared` and the save schema for negative content value; retheming gives an entire authored frost region an element and costs three field edits.
7. **Reserve: implement or retract?** → **Implement, minimally.** NG+ is mandated, and NG+ without `reserve` silently drops characters. Populate it in #133, add a swap action to the party overlay, and skip a dedicated screen.
8. **Quest failure: implement or delete?** → **Implement, narrowly.** Three or four main seeds with a `flag_set` condition and a recovery quest. `GAME_DESIGN.md:40` sells it as the most distinctive story promise, the engine functions already exist and are tested, and deleting means a migration anyway. Do **not** build time-limit failure — it fights the free-roam structure.
9. **Can a quest decision skip steps (`skipToStep`) or only change consequences?** → **Consequences only in v1.** It covers three of the four promised cases at a fraction of the risk; add `skipToStep` in a second pass if a specific quest needs it.
10. **How many quests get decisions?** → **The four whose summaries already promise a choice**, plus the two companion-recruitment quests. Six. Do not add more until they play well.
11. **How many new objective kinds?** → **Exactly two: `deliver` and `survive`.** Re-author ~10 of the 15 regional seeds onto them. Escort/timed stay out — they need scene-level AI the simplicity law refuses.
12. **Delete or implement the `"resolving"` phase?** → **Delete.** It matches actual behaviour and `BattleScene` already has a working local flag. Implementing only pays off if the initiative rework needs it, and it does not.

### B. Balance (Wave 3 — one tuning commit)

13. **Should under-levelled players be soft-blocked or merely punished?** → **Punished, no floor.** Authored encounter levels with no party-relative floor. A floor reintroduces exactly the "leveling makes you weaker" inversion #9 exists to kill.
14. **Target boss fight length, and does the engine damage formula move?** → **8-12 rounds; move enemy stat scaling first, formula second.** Try `EngineGameBridge.ts:574` alone; only touch `combat.ts:130`'s defence coefficient if stat scaling cannot get there without absurd HP numbers. Keep `combat.ts` as pure and as stable as possible.
15. **Move the XP curve or the rewards?** → **Move the rewards.** Changing `totalExperienceForLevel` changes what a stored `experience` value means in every existing save. Superlinear reward keeps saves meaningful.
16. **Should hard difficulty grant more XP?** → **No — reduce income on hard.** Currently the tightest setting is also the richest and finishes ~4 levels ahead, which is backwards. Scale currency and drops down; keep XP flat across difficulties so all three land in the same authored band.
17. **How much status pressure to push onto the player in #40?** → **Moderate: two of the eleven trash enemies per region get a DoT, one gets a turn-denial.** Enough to make Ash Spice and Guard worth buying; not enough to make a cure-item stockpile mandatory. Re-check after #53 lands, since DoT scaling changes the answer.
18. **Percentage-of-maxHp poison — capped or resisted?** → **Resisted via `statusResistance`, and capped at 8% of maxHp per tick.** Uncapped percentage damage trivialises the boss HP you just spent #41 raising.
19. **Does freeze's shatter bonus apply to all damage or physical only?** → **Physical only.** Gives freeze a distinct identity against burn (magic-flavoured) and keeps the three turn-denials differentiated.
20. **Is group healing in the first AoE pass?** → **No, defer.** Single-target-vs-group offence is the decision players actually feel; group healing is a balance problem on top of a balance problem.
21. **Should escape fail at all, in an opt-in encounter model?** → **It should cost a round, not fail.** Escape resolves but the enemy phase runs first. Preserves the opt-in design's intent while making attrition real.
22. **May an incapacitated character use a cure item on themselves?** → **No — require an unafflicted ally.** Once item targeting exists (#37) this is the interesting rule, and it is the whole reason `root_party`/`scorch_party` boss phases exist.
23. **Reward-seed entropy: `worldMinutes` or a persisted victory counter?** → **`worldMinutes`.** It already seeds combat at `:1017`, adds no world flag, and stays fully replayable from a save.

### C. Content scope (Waves 4-5)

24. **Do advanced branches contribute their own level-up skill entries after L4?** → **Yes, 2 entries each.** It is the payoff for the branch choice becoming irreversible in #73, and it makes twelve branches feel like twelve builds.
25. **Does ancestry modify stat growth, or only job?** → **Job only.** Two growth axes multiply the tuning surface for a difference the player cannot perceive. Ancestry's differentiation comes from #136's exclusive skill instead.
26. **Camp item vs hard town-only rest?** → **Town-gated paid rest plus an authored camp consumable for wilderness/dungeon.** Town-only alone risks a 0-currency 0-MP softlock deep in a dungeon; the consumable is the anti-softlock floor and gives the shop a recurring sale.
27. **Should Rest keep clearing all statuses for free once a paid cure exists?** → **Paid rest clears statuses; free field-rest does not.** That is exactly what makes Ash Spice worth carrying.
28. **How many equipment pieces per slot per region?** → **Three.** Two makes the "tradeoff, not strict upgrade" rule impossible to express; four explodes authoring. Three = drop tier, purchasable tier, sidegrade.
29. **How restrictive should `allowedJobIds` be?** → **Two bands only: martial and caster.** Per-job restriction on a catalog this size makes half of it unwearable. Resolve to the base job via `baseJobIdFor` — do not enumerate branch ids.
30. **Charisma: shop discount or relationship bonus?** → **Shop discount.** Smaller, deterministic, and it lands exactly when #90 makes marks scarce. A relationship bonus would need a second tuning pass on faction thresholds.
31. **Prices up or rewards down for the economy rebalance?** → **Prices up.** Keeps the engine untouched and keeps existing saves' currency meaningful.
32. **Split the repeatable-encounter find table, or leave it?** → **Split.** Keep quest-collect materials repeatable (`what-the-tree-forgot` needs 7 memory-shards); gate scripted one-time finds behind the `findFlag` pattern `travel()` already uses.
33. **Should narrative key items persist as mementos after their quest resolves?** → **Yes for `severance-ledger` and `star-key`; consume everything else.** Add an explicit `memento: true` flag rather than special-casing ids in the bridge.
34. **How many new locations for the world graph?** → **Four: one per region plus one cross-region shortcut.** Enough to create two real cycles; bounded authoring.
35. **Is the Quick Encounter key an exploit or an accessibility affordance?** → **Accessibility affordance — keep it, gate it behind a settings flag, rename the action.** Removing it outright costs players who cannot navigate a grid; leaving it on by default makes #106 pointless.
36. **Is fast travel town-only or any discovered location?** → **Town-only.** Keeps dungeon traversal meaningful, which is the entire point of #108 and #109.
37. **Does any NPC get an hourly schedule?** → **No — ship the day-phase tint only.** The tint fixes the credibility problem at a fraction of the cost, and schedules risk a location with zero NPCs at some hour.
38. **Opened-chest state: `world.flags` or a new persisted field?** → **`world.flags`.** Consistent with the existing `findFlag` pattern and needs no migration.
39. **Which quests get `fear` consequences?** → **The Concord coercion branch plus any quest resolved by threat** — a WORLD_BIBLE tone call, not a code one. Assign it to whoever owns the world bible before Wave 5 starts.
40. **Prologue: separate `IntroScene` or in-world beat renderer?** → **In-world beat renderer, with the title card as a full-screen beat kind.** Less code, one renderer to maintain, and the card limitation is solvable with a beat variant.
41. **Free-text character naming?** → **No.** Keep the curated list; render it as suggestions. Free text complicates the AI prompt surface and the accessibility announcements for a cosmetic gain.

### D. Endgame and replay (Wave 6)

42. **How many postgame quests?** → **Five: one hub quest, three ending-gated, one superboss unlock.** Enough to make `ending.*` flags mean something without a second campaign.
43. **Is the postgame flag one boolean or per-ending?** → **One boolean plus the existing `ending.*` flags.** Two mechanisms for one state invites drift.
44. **Is ending-gated content postgame-only or does it alter the last act?** → **Postgame-only.** Altering the last act multiplies QA on the three branches for a payoff most players see once.
45. **Is the superboss repeatable?** → **Repeatable.** It is the only remaining combat loop and NG+ replay needs a target. Gate its unique drop behind a one-time flag.
46. **What carries over in NG+?** → **Levels, skills, job unlocks, equipment, and the chronicle. Not currency, not consumables, not quest progress, not key items.** Carrying currency plus gear plus a tuned economy makes the early game trivial twice over; carrying growth is what players actually want.
47. **Can NG+ select a new difficulty?** → **Yes.** The draft already carries difficulty and it is the main reason to replay. Record the cleared difficulty and ending as world flags so the title screen can show them.
48. **Should the point of no return block entry, or only warn?** → **Warn only, but name outstanding quest count and unspent marks.** Blocking is paternalistic in a game whose endings are the advertised replay driver; an informed commit is the genre norm.
49. **Which single mid-game unlock?** → **`requiresFlag` on `WorldRoute`, one route in the Cinder March, opened by a main-quest key item.** Engine owns the check, content owns the data, and it is the only option that does not touch the combat core and its tests.
50. **Does the codex gate a first-battle prompt?** → **No — opt-in from the menu, plus a one-time toast on first battle pointing at it.** A forced tutorial in a game this short is friction.

### E. Engineering and platform

51. **Default key for quickload?** → **F9** (genre convention), behind a confirm prompt so it never silently discards progress. **Does the quick slot show in the load menu?** → **Yes**, as a fourth row.
52. **WASD: implement or retract?** → **Implement**, as `Record<KeyboardAction, readonly string[]>` with a `SETTINGS_STORAGE_KEY` bump to `.v2`. It is the first thing every player tries, it is documented, and the existing unknown-version-resets path handles the upgrade for free. Retracting is cheaper but leaves the game feeling wrong.
53. **Negotiation (GAME_DESIGN.md:9): implement or retract?** → **Retract.** It is a whole second resolution system for a nine-encounter campaign, and #16's quest decisions deliver the "choices matter" promise more cheaply. Strike the line.
54. **Are gamepad bindings rebindable?** → **No.** Fixed mapping with the two free shoulder buttons assigned. A second binding map and capture mode is real scope for a small audience.
55. **Is 9px HUD text raised outright, independent of the text-scale setting?** → **Yes — floor it at 11px.** A setting should improve a legible baseline, not rescue an illegible one.
56. **Is mobile/touch a supported target?** → **No.** Drop the `max-width: 720px` media query and render a DOM "keyboard or gamepad required" note. The project has no touch input and adding an on-screen d-pad is a feature, not a fix.
57. **Should a content-pack version mismatch reconcile silently or refuse and offer the backup?** → **Reconcile, then surface a one-line notice.** Refusing strands the player behind a decision they cannot evaluate; the backup remains available via #129 either way.
58. **Job unlocks: engine-owned prerequisites or bridge-owned flags?** → **Engine-owned.** Product law names the engine as the owner of progression, the engine's model is strictly richer (structured blocker reasons the UI can render), and 145 lines of tested code already exist. This also decides that `unlockedJobIds` lives in `GameState`.
59. **Is real wall-clock play time worth persisting?** → **No.** Rename `playTimeMinutes` → `worldMinutes` so the field stops lying, and stop there.
60. **When does `EngineGameBridge.ts` get split?** → **Wave 6, after the churn stops, data-extraction phase only.** Move `COMBAT_SKILLS`, job/ancestry stat tables, recovery items, sprite/tint tables and `CONCORD_CHOICES` into `src/content/`; move `applyBossPhaseTransitions` into `src/engine/combat.ts`. Do not attempt a general architectural split — what remains after those two moves is genuinely integration.

## B3. Softlocks and input locks

The broad softlock prompt stalled five consecutive times; re-split into three file-scoped agents (`battle-escape`, `quest-deadend`, `input-lock`) it completed in 26 minutes. 11 findings, **1 refuted, 10 survived** — and the reproduction pass did real work here: it **corrected severity downward on 8 of 10**, killing three separate "permanently locks the player out" claims by finding the recovery route the auditor missed. ### Refuted

- **Killing a named boss before its quest step reaches it destroys the main story chain.** `ALREADY_HANDLED`. The engine-layer mechanism is accurate — `applyQuestObjective` (`quests.ts:64-66`) credits a `defeat` event only when it matches the *current* step, `resolveVictory` appends to `defeatedBossIds` unconditionally (`:1431-1433`), `startEncounter` hard-returns for an already-defeated boss (`:1002`), and there is no rescan. But the content makes the ordering unreachable: the quest that requires each boss is already active before that boss's encounter becomes available. Worth knowing this is content-guarded rather than code-guarded — a future content edit could expose it.

### What this sweep cleared

Recording these so nobody re-audits them: escape from non-boss fights works correctly and cannot strand the party; `addOrRefreshStatus` replaces rather than accumulates, so status duration cannot be extended indefinitely; quest counters use `>=` and cannot overflow past their target; `WorldScene`'s overlay state machine always has a recovery route because `closeOverlay()` unconditionally clears `locked` and `toggleOverlay("system")` does not check it.
