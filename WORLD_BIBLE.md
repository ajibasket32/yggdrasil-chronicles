# World Bible

## Canon statement

Yggdrasil Chronicles is an original fantasy setting drawing only on the public-domain mythic idea of a world tree. It does not share characters, guilds, items, spells, terminology, plotlines, or visual identity with any existing franchise.

## The world tree

Yggdrasil is not a god but a continent-spanning living phenomenon whose buried rootways carry luminous aether. Civilizations grew around places where roots surface, using their resin-glass to preserve memory. The tree is weakening: entire rootways have gone silent, and memories stored in them are returning altered.

## Present age

The game begins in the Year 417 of the Rootbound Concord. The Concord once guaranteed free travel and shared stewardship of the rootways. It is now collapsing under resource scarcity, old grievances, and evidence that somebody is cutting living roots beneath the frontier.

## Playable regions

### The Verdant Reach

A rain-bright frontier of orchard towns, flooded ruins, and exposed roots. Hearthcross is the starting settlement. The region values mutual obligation but hides the cost of its prosperity.

### The Cinder March

Basalt valleys and wind-carved foundries surrounding Emberwake. Guilds compete to refine resin-glass while ash spirits gather in abandoned kilns.

### The Pale Canopy

A high, cold forest where white branches form bridges above a buried city. The observatory settlement Larkspire records stars that no longer match the oldest root memories.

## Peoples

- Hearthborn: adaptable communities shaped by trade and migration.
- Sylvans: long-lived listeners trained to read changes in living wood.
- Stonekin: dense-boned makers whose craft binds mineral and root resin.
- Wayfarers: mixed-heritage descendants of the original Concord caravans.

These are cultures and ancestries, not moral alignments.

## Factions

- Rootwardens protect living rootways and restrict dangerous excavation.
- The Brass Compact controls refinement, transport, and mercenary contracts.
- Lantern Archive preserves memories and investigates contradictions.
- Freebound Companies are independent explorers with competing codes.
- The Quiet Choir believes the tree must forget the present age to survive.

## Magic

Magic shapes aether through practiced forms: ember, tide, gale, stone, frost, storm, radiance, shadow, nature, and unbound aether. Magic cannot create truth from nothing; divination interprets stored impressions and can be mistaken.

## Immutable rules

Time moves forward. Named deaths matter. Knowledge is local and fallible. Power has a visible cost. Choices close as well as open paths. The world existed before the player and does not revolve around them.

## Originality exclusions

Do not introduce franchise-specific ranks, spell names, world-class artifacts, transported-player plots, copied factions, protected characters, or recognizable story beats. New canon must pass the same originality check as authored content.
